All Marvel Rivals Season 9 Team Ups
By
Craig Robinson
Senior Writer
Last Updated:
13/07/2026
With the release of Marvel Rivals Season 9, the developers restructured how its team-up system works. Previously, players had to select two heroes that had a team up for the season, and then could get the benefits of that team up. Instead, NetEase Games is trialling something different, where every hero gets two baseline team up effects to choose from. Then, if that heroes is in the game, it gets a slightly better effect to make it stronger.
The new system should encourage more players to try out heroes and new combo abilities, without needing to force someone to play a pick they otherwise might now want. And, it also further encourages players to still team up if they want to. Here’s a closer look at all the Marvel Rivals Season 9 team ups.
If you need more Season 9 Marvel Rivals content, check out the
Death of Apocalypse event solutions
.
All Marvel Rivals Season 9 Team Ups

Reddit researchers on this doc

release patch notes
, with the likes of Iron Man’s x Thor effect getting nerfed.
